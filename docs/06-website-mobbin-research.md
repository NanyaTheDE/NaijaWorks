# Website research (Mobbin)

Five Mobbin section searches for the marketing website in `website/`: hero, how it works, stats, feature grid and testimonials. Each finding below says what we changed, or why we didn't.

## 1. Stats: say what the number means before showing it

| Reference | What it does |
|---|---|
| [YLLW](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0d46242a-8fae-4e37-8baf-c13ac408bd2e) | A heading ("must be measurable") over 4 tall cards. Each card has a label on top and a big number at the bottom. |
| [Robot.com](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f85060f2-e490-4793-b11f-86d41a6a2cd0), [COLLINS](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a34730b6-df7e-43e5-8e7d-05b11fe6f8c4) | The same card layout, with a sentence of context under the label. |

**Gap:** the home stats were a thin centred strip with a short label under each number, so "6" or "1.9M" didn't explain itself.
**Change:** a "Progress you can count." heading, a link to how we count and the data limits, and 4 tall cards. Each card has a label, one line of context and the number at the bottom. One card is dark to break up the row.

## 2. Product demo: name the steps

| Reference | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Beside](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/03f765e3-3432-4ac7-ab08-a240654f65db) | A row of named feature tabs above the phone mockup. |
| [Beside, live demo](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/bacfcd5d-bcca-4640-8fcf-da2f03dc8669) | "Don't take our word for it. Give it a try now", with a small mono label. |

**Gap:** under the hero phone, the tracker demo used unlabelled dots plus arrows, so you couldn't tell there were 7 stages or jump to "Fixed".
**Change:** a "Live demo · tap a stage" label and a strip of named stage chips (with icons) that scrolls the active stage into view. Past stages turn green. The prev/next arrows are gone because the chips do that job.

## 3. Hero: a label above the strip under the hero

| Reference | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Partiful](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/5472e9d8-de81-40dc-ba9e-a2d6f4e71fe1), [Intercom](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c1289578-0396-4d90-bb1b-8c6975b09101), [Tailscale](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/e72d1d36-a761-4557-b655-d2797beb7abb) | A small uppercase label ("As featured in", "Trusted by…") above a logo row right under the hero. |

**Change:** a "Report anything public that's broken" label above the category marquee. We don't show agency or press logos: we have no real partners, and fake logos would imitate real organisations.

## 4. Government features: one big card with product UI

| Reference | What it does |
|---|---|
| [FLORA](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/27fbff58-42db-49e5-84c9-0a9ff1bcca07) | One large card showing the dashboard, with three smaller feature cards under it. |
| [Windsurf](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/578c6a44-919f-4628-8573-42ba22a389d7), [Retool](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/0daad8f7-cdde-4ef2-a454-2a26d2bbe0a5) | Cards of mixed sizes, each with a small piece of product UI. |

**Gap:** on `/for-government`, six feature cards of the same size made the main idea (a triage queue, not a complaint inbox) look no more important than the others.
**Change:** a bento grid. The triage card is dark and spans 2×2, with a small queue mock sorted by risk and impact. In it, an open manhole with 2 reports sits above a faded zebra crossing with 200, which shows "severity beats popularity". The other five cards stay small.

## 5. How it works and testimonials: no change

[Clay](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/28c16d19-1b1c-4c38-b06e-660c77901cbc) and [ClassPass](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/193363c3-3373-49a5-a1d0-00ff01f07b4d) use numbered step cards, and [Function](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/7c72acdf-c8a3-425c-b7a6-2e0c487bb17c) uses large "Step 01" blocks. Our three step cards, each with a small UI preview, already do this. [Oyster](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1b42e29a-f1e2-43ff-a249-0f2494e473e2) and [Cursor](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/146dd81b-925f-479d-b55c-2677c86f2fdd) use quote cards with an avatar, name and role, which matches ours.
